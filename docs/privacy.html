Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

DevMop runs entirely on your Mac. Nothing you scan or clean is ever sent to us or anyone else.

What leaves your Mac

Nothing. DevMop has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or network requests of any kind.

What it reads

Folder and file names, sizes and dates, so it can show where space goes. It doesn’t open your documents, mail or messages. To find exact duplicates it compares file contents, on your Mac only.

Why it asks for Full Disk Access

macOS protects Library, Mail, Messages, Photos and the Trash. Without access DevMop can’t measure them, and simply skips them. You can turn access off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access.

Tools it runs

git, xcrun simctl, docker, brew, pnpm, npm, tmutil, diskutil, lsof and qlmanage — to read information, or to clean when you confirm. They run on your Mac and are bound by their own privacy terms.

What it stores

  • Your settings, in ~/Library/Preferences/app.devmop.DevMop.plist.
  • The last scan, a history of cleanups and cleanup baselines, in ~/Library/Application Support/DevMop.

Delete either at any time; DevMop starts fresh.

This website

This site is a set of static pages and sets no cookies. It counts visits and clicks on the Download buttons with GoatCounter, which stores no personal data and doesn’t track you across sites; if your browser sends Do Not Track, you aren’t counted at all. The number of downloads comes from GitHub Releases.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request information such as IP addresses under GitHub’s privacy statement.

None of this applies to the app: DevMop itself never sends anything.

Children

DevMop collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. If DevMop ever needs to send any data, this page will say so first.
