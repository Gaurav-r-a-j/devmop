Using DevMop

DevMop helps you find and remove files on your Mac. You decide what is removed: nothing is deleted until you review the list and confirm it. You’re responsible for what you choose to remove.

Keep backups

DevMop moves most items to the Trash and checks each one again before removing it, but no software is perfect. Keep a current backup, for example with Time Machine, before cleaning anything you can’t easily get back.
